THE BATTLE BEGINS

RoboKriti 2026 is an inter-class robotics competition where students come together to build, compete and put their robotics skills to the test. Organised by the Tinkering & Robotics Lab of Army Public School, Lal Bahadur Shastri Marg, Lucknow, RoboKriti brings together engineering, strategy, teamwork and competitive spirit.
